## Lv1 – The Waiting Room

*(khi người chơi chạm NPC ở cuối màn)*

```
...Can you hear me...?

Don’t be afraid. I won’t hurt you.

I’ve been here... for such a long time.

Waiting. Hoping someone would listen.

You stopped for me.

Thank you.
```

## Lv2 – The Broken Promise

*(khi đến đích/NPC ở cuối màn)*

```
You came back...

I didn’t think anyone would.

I remember now...

I promised I’d never leave them behind.

But I did.

That promise... it kept me here.
```

---

## Lv3 – The Locked Door

*(khi đến chạm NPC ở cuối màn)*

```
You're still here...

Most people turn away.

I tried to forget.

I tried to lock everything away.

But my regrets... they built these walls.

And I was trapped inside them.
```

---

## Lv4 – The Final Farewell

*(khi chạm NPC ở cuối màn, giải thoát)*

```
This is the last place...

Where I should have said goodbye.

But I was too scared.

Thank you... for walking with me this far.

I think... I can go now.

Goodbye.
```
